The lotus is one of the most beautiful flowers in the world.
It grows in ponds, lakes, and other freshwater places.
Its petals are usually pink, white, or light red in color.
The flower opens during the daytime and closes at night.
The lotus has broad green leaves that float on the water.
It is the national flower of India and represents purity and wisdom.
Many people use lotus flowers for worship and decoration.
The flower attracts bees and butterflies with its sweet fragrance.
Every part of the lotus plant is useful, including its seeds and roots.
The lotus teaches us to stay pure and strong even in difficult situations.
